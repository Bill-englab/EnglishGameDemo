# My English Adventure Domain Language

This glossary defines the curriculum language used across product, content, and implementation work.

## Curriculum Structure

**Curriculum**:
The complete 4–6-year-old English role-play learning journey.
_Avoid_: Course, syllabus, program when referring to the whole journey

**Stage**:
One developmental part of the Curriculum, presented as Stage 1, 2, or 3 with a communication focus; approximate ages 4, 5, and 6 guide content design rather than admission or automatic promotion.
_Avoid_: Level, grade, proficiency score

**Phase**:
A developmental segment within one Stage that groups Chapters with a shared growth focus.
_Avoid_: Stage, term

**Chapter**:
A map world organized around one family of real-life communication needs.
_Avoid_: Grammar unit, topic when referring to the map hierarchy

**Lesson**:
One map node built around a concrete communication task and its demo.
_Avoid_: Session, dialogue, course, level in curriculum documentation

**Session**:
One real practice encounter with a Lesson; the same Lesson is normally practiced in three different Sessions.
_Avoid_: Lesson, level

**Peer**:
The child's one recurring same-age conversation partner across play, negotiation, and relationship-repair Lessons.
_Avoid_: Classmate, friend name, or a specific animal when referring to the curriculum role

## Learning Design

**Can-Do**:
The real-life communication task the child can complete with age-appropriate support after a Lesson.
_Avoid_: Grammar objective, mastery score

**Conversation Move**:
The communicative action taken in a turn, such as requesting, refusing, clarifying, repairing, or proposing.
_Avoid_: Sentence pattern when the intent is broader than one wording

**Trigger**:
The partner's utterance or action that creates a reason for the child to respond.
_Avoid_: Question, cue when it may also be a non-question action

**Core Response**:
The shortest natural response that completes the Lesson's Can-Do.
_Avoid_: Easy answer, low-level answer

**Stretch Response**:
A natural extension of the Core Response that adds one reason, condition, or useful detail.
_Avoid_: Advanced answer, higher score

**Repair Response**:
Language used when the child did not understand, misspoke, or was misunderstood.
_Avoid_: Correction, wrong-answer fix

**Recycle**:
Intentional reuse of a previously introduced Conversation Move in a new context.
_Avoid_: Review when the expression is actively used to accomplish a new task

**Replay Card**:
A complete alternate role-play situation that preserves the Lesson's Conversation Move while changing the context.
_Avoid_: Variation string, word replacement

## Media and Progress

**Demo**:
The adult-prepared model video for one Lesson.
_Avoid_: Teaching video, answer video

**Performance**:
The child's saved role-play recording and the binary evidence that a Lesson was completed.
_Avoid_: Test, submission, assessment

**Content Revision**:
The version of a Lesson's meaning-bearing content used to determine whether its Demo still matches.
_Avoid_: App version, file timestamp

**Stale Demo**:
A Demo produced from an older Content Revision and therefore waiting for human review or replacement.
_Avoid_: Broken demo, failed demo
