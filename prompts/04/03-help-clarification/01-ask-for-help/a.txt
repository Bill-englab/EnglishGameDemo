SETTING

One large paper star outline lies at center-right, visibly already cut around almost its whole perimeter except one short final edge; leave a small curved attachment requiring only one or two snips, not a whole star to cut in ten seconds. One pair of blunt child-safe scissors lies closed at table-left within Dad's easy reach but outside Child's comfortable reach. Keep one locked 16:9 medium two-shot showing faces and hands. Dad never takes over the cutting; fingers remain well clear of the blades.

START FRAME
Dad and Child sit at the table; the partly cut paper star lies at center-right with its final edge attached, and the closed child-safe scissors lie at table-left.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
Dad indicates the unfinished paper outline during the question. Child looks from the outline toward the scissors and asks for help retrieving them. Dad prepares to reach for them only after the request; no cutting yet.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Dad: "You want to cut out this paper star. What do you need?"
Child: "Can you help me get my child-safe scissors?"

END FRAME
