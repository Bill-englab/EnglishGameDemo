SETTING
Looking for a toy car beside a low shelf at home.

START FRAME
Dad kneels screen-left and Child screen-right beside the low shelf; both have empty hands. Child looks over the empty rug. The blue box stands at the shelf's front-right, hiding the stationary red car with black wheels at the back-right. Child wears his yellow T-shirt, navy shorts and cream socks.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
Dad notices Child searching the rug and asks what is missing. Child looks up, asks about his red car and mentions having it at story time. Dad asks where he played with it last. Child remembers the rug and putting it near the shelf, glancing from the rug to the low shelf during his answer. Neither character touches the box or car.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Dad: "You keep looking around. What's missing?"
Child: "Where's my red car? I had it at story time."
Dad: "Where did you play with it last?"
Child: "On the rug. Then I put it near the shelf."
