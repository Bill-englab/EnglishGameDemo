ACTION AND REACTIONS
Teacher presents the found bottle and asks who owns it, without handing it over. Child indicates the yellow star and makes the initial claim. Teacher keeps both bottles in view; do not treat the shared star as enough evidence of ownership.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Teacher: "This water bottle was left by the art table. Whose is it?"
Child: "It's mine. It has a yellow star."
