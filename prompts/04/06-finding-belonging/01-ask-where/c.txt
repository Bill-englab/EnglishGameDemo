No screaming, extreme excitement, rage, distorted facial or body shapes, frantic gestures or uncontrolled running.

SETTING
Looking for a toy car beside a low shelf at home.

START FRAME
Dad and Child remain kneeling screen-left and screen-right, now looking toward the space behind the blue box with empty hands. The blue box remains at the shelf's front-right, the red car with black wheels is hidden at the back-right, and the space to the box's left is clear. Child's outfit is unchanged.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
During his opening turn Dad uses his right hand to slide the blue box a short distance left into the already clear shelf space, visibly revealing the red car without touching it. Child recognizes the car, reaches with his right hand and lifts that same car from the back-right of the shelf, reporting where it was. Dad acknowledges the find with a small smile while Child brings it close. Dad releases the box and returns his right hand to his lap. These small actions overlap the speech; neither character stands or starts another game.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Dad: "Let's move the box and see."
Child: "There it is! My red car was under the box."
