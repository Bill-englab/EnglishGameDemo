ACTION AND REACTIONS
Child points once to the blue box while naming both box jobs — blue for blocks, red for cars. Dad approves the plan and invites the blocks. Child lifts one block with each hand in turn and places both into the blue box, finishing the group before reporting it done and naming the red car as his next job. SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):

Dad: "Good plan. Put the two blocks in."
Child: "Done. Now the cars. I'll take the red one."
