ACTION AND REACTIONS

Child makes one or two short snips to free the last edge, then closes and lays the scissors at table-right before speaking proudly. Child lifts the freed slightly wavy star; Dad keeps the surrounding scrap on the table. Do not cut a full star at impossible speed.

Child: "It is a little wiggly, but it is still my star!"
