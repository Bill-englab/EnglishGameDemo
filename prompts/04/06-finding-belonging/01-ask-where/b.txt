A locked 16:9 medium two-shot includes faces, table, and this low search area.

START FRAME
Child's hands are empty above the table and both look toward the sofa; the red piece remains beside its front leg.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
Dad gives his truthful clue with a small gesture toward the sofa. Child follows the gesture and indicates the gap below the sofa while requesting help. After the request, both lean slightly toward that same front leg; do not reveal or retrieve the piece before Dad's next turn.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Dad: "I saw a red piece near the sofa."
Child: "Can you help me look under the sofa?"

END FRAME
Both lean toward the sofa's near front leg; Child's empty right hand rests beside the table and the red piece is still on the floor by the leg.
