ACTION AND REACTIONS
Mom checks the near shelf and notes the missing oatmeal. Child looks at the same shelf, then asks Mom where to find it. Neither can yet see, identify, or reach for a blue box; the camera continuously shows both boxes fixed on the adjacent shelf beyond the panel that blocks only the characters' sightline.

Child: "Where can we find the oatmeal?"
