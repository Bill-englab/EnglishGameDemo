ACTION AND REACTIONS
Mom names the dinner need, and Child asks her where to look before scanning the shelf. Mom gives the bottom-shelf clue with a small glance toward it, keeping the basket steady in her right hand. Child follows her gaze to the already visible red packet. The item was never concealed; the question directs his search. Neither character steps away or touches the bag yet.

Child: "Where can we find the pasta?"
Mom: "It's on the bottom shelf."
