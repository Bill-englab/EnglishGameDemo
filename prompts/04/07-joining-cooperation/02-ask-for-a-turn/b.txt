START FRAME
Peer controls the truck a short distance from the open box with both tan blocks still loaded; Child waits beside the two blue blocks.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
Peer indicates the two loaded tan blocks as the agreed endpoint. During Child's confirmation, Peer rolls the truck the final few centimeters, lifts each tan block once, and places both visibly inside the box. Do not hand over the truck until this two-block job is finished.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Peer: "Yes, after I carry these two blocks."
Child: "Okay. You carry two blocks, then it is my turn."

END FRAME
Both tan blocks are inside the box; Peer holds the empty red truck beside it and Child waits with the two blue blocks untouched.
