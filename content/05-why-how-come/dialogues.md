# 第 5 类：为什么 / 怎么

**覆盖高频功能：** 质疑规则、追原因、问方法——4 岁问不停的核心。

---

## D1｜问规则原因

**背景：** 叫他刷牙，他追问几次。

**目标句式：** `Why do I have to ___?`（为什么得…）

> **F:** It's time to brush your teeth now.
> **C:** **Why do I have to** brush them two times?
> **F:** Because little sugar bugs are hiding on your teeth. Brushing chases them away.
> **C:** But I brushed them last night!
> **F:** The bugs come back while you sleep. That's why we brush every morning too.
> **C:** And **why do I have to** use the toothpaste too?
> **F:** Toothpaste makes your teeth strong and shiny. The bugs can't hold on.
> **C:** Okay! Bye-bye, sugar bugs!

**换样重演：** "brush twice" 换 "go to bed now"；"toothpaste" 换 "the foam one"。

---

## D2｜问原因

**背景：** 今晚没甜点，他追问。

**目标句式：** `How come ___?`（怎么/为什么·口语）

> **F:** We are not having any dessert tonight.
> **C:** **How come** we can't have dessert?
> **F:** Your tummy is still full of party cake and candy.
> **C:** But that was a long time ago!
> **F:** The sugar is still in there, bouncing around. It will keep you awake at bedtime.
> **C:** **How come** sugar keeps me awake?
> **F:** It makes your body want to jump and play, even in bed.
> **C:** Okay. Can I have dessert tomorrow, then?

**换样重演：** "dessert" 换 "ice cream"；"cake and candy" 换 "birthday cake"。

---

## D3｜问方法

**背景：** 他问爸爸怎么预判下雨。

**目标句式：** `How do you ___?`（怎么做）· `Why does ___?`（为什么…）

> **C:** **How do you** know that it's going to rain?
> **F:** Look up. The clouds are getting dark and heavy.
> **C:** **Why does** the sky look like that?
> **F:** The clouds are full of water. When they get too heavy, down comes the rain.
> **C:** **How do you** know so many sky things?
> **F:** I watch the sky every day on my way to work.
> **C:** So the sky is like a giant puddle up there?
> **F:** Sort of! Grab your coat, just in case.

**换样重演：** "rain" 换 "get dark"；"clouds" 换 "wind"。
