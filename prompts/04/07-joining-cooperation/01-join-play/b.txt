START FRAME

ACTION AND REACTIONS
Child offers to make the bridge connection with a small gesture toward his blue piece. Peer checks whether it will connect to the road; he does not agree before Child answers. Child points along the road's continuation, leaving room for the remaining tan piece and then the bridge. Only after that clarification does Peer give a small accepting nod. Both pieces stay on the mat until the shared plan is agreed.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):

Peer: "Will it join my road?"
Child: "Yes, right here."
Peer: "Okay, let's build."
