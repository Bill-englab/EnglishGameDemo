An unfastened lightweight coat hangs open over the bench end nearest the door, within Dad's reach. Child wears the usual yellow T-shirt underneath. START FRAME
Child sits with both slip-on shoes on; the bag remains beside the bench and the open coat still rests over the bench end by the door; Dad kneels left.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
During Dad's turn Child stands steadily in both shoes while Dad lifts the open coat from the bench and presents its sleeves. Child threads both arms into the already open sleeves with Dad's help. Only once the coat is on does Child say the final readiness line. Leave the coat unfastened; no rushed zipper or button action.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):
Dad: "Both shoes are on. Your coat is by the door."
Child: "Now my coat is on. I am ready."

END FRAME
Child stands screen-right in both shoes and the open coat over the yellow T-shirt; Dad remains left with empty hands; the bag stays beside the bench and they are ready to leave.
