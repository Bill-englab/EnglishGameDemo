START FRAME
Mom holds the folded cold cloth in her right hand, ready beside Child's still right knee; her left hand rests lightly on the sofa beside him; Child watches calmly with hands in his lap.

ACTION AND REACTIONS
With his agreement, Mom gently lays the soft cloth against the front of the same right knee and holds it lightly during her line. Do not press, rub or use ice directly. Child stays comfortably seated, relaxes slightly and reports that the cloth feels nice with a small quiet smile of relief, without implying the pain is gone. Play does not resume.

SPOKEN DIALOGUE (verbatim, in order):

Child: "The cloth feels nice. Thank you, Mom."
